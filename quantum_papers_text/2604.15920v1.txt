Title: Local qubit invariants on quantum computer
Authors: Szilárd Szalay, Frédéric Holweck
Categories: quant-ph, math-ph
Published: 2026-04-17 10:21:32+00:00
Abstract: We present two general methods to implement quantum circuits for the direct measuring of local unitary invariants on quantum computers. We work these out for important three-qubit invariants, and also demonstrate these on the IBM Quantum Platform for important entanglement measures of three qubits.
