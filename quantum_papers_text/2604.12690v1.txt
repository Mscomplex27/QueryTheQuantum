Title: An Introduction to Quantum Graphs and Current Applications
Authors: Gregory Berkolaiko, Sven Gnutzmann
Categories: quant-ph, math-ph
Published: 2026-04-14 13:00:51+00:00
Abstract: Quantum graphs are a paradigmatic model for quantum chaos as well as for spectral theory.
  We give a concise didactical introduction to quantum graphs, or Schrödinger Hamiltonians on
  metric graphs, with a focus on results related
  to quantum chaos, periodic orbit theory and spectral theory. We summarise related
  seminal results, and give an overview over a few more recent developments.
