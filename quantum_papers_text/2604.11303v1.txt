Title: Low-dose Image Recognition with Quantum Computational Electron Microscopy
Authors: Hiroshi Okamoto
Categories: quant-ph
Published: 2026-04-13 11:01:32+00:00
Abstract: We show that quantum computational imaging is advantageous in the setting of low-dose electron microscopy of beam-sensitive specimens. Two qudits placed near the electron beam enable full transfer of quantum information between the electron microscope and a quantum computer in the proposed scheme, providing the specimen is a phase object. We present a quantum algorithm that identifies the correct image among n candidate images, where n is larger than the effective dimension of the Hilbert space of the imaging electron.
