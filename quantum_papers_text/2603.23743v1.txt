Title: On Sub-Sevenfold Symmetries in LH2 Stacked Ring Scaffolds: A Quantum Optical Perspective
Authors: Arpita Pal
Categories: physics.optics, quant-ph
Published: 2026-03-24 22:05:09+00:00
Abstract: Using a closed quantum optical coupled-dipole model, we investigate why sub-sevenfold symmetries are likely absent in the stacked-ring scaffolds of light-harvesting 2 (LH2) complexes in purple photosynthetic bacteria.
