Title: The force of attraction between nucleons due to vacuum fluctuation
Authors: Anupam Ghosh
Categories: hep-ph, hep-th, nucl-th, quant-ph
Published: 2026-03-30 12:45:48+00:00
Abstract: In this letter, we derive the interaction energy and the force between two parallel metal plates, arising from quantum vacuum fluctuations when they are very close to each other. We consider the vacuum to be composed of a meson field. In Quantum Hadrodynamics, mesons are the carriers of the nuclear force.
