Title: Circuit Optimization for Universality Transformation
Authors: Yasuaki Nakayama, Yuki Takeuchi, Seiseki Akibue
Categories: quant-ph
Published: 2026-03-13 17:03:44+00:00
Abstract: It is known that a computationally universal gate set $\{H,CCZ\}$ can be transformed to a strictly universal one $\{H, Λ(S)\}$ using one maximally imaginary state $|+i \rangle$ and non-imaginary ancillary qubits. We succeed this transformation with a shorter circuit that eliminates non-imaginary ancillary qubits. We further extend this to the continuous gate-set setting, showing that any multi-qubit unitary can be exactly generated by real single-qubit unitary gates, $CCZ$ gates and $|0 \rangle |+i \rangle$.
