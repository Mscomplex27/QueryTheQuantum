Title: Topological robustness of orbital angular momentum entanglement in stochastic channels
Authors: Tatjana Kleine, Pedro Ornelas, Cade Peters, Zhenyu Guo, Bereneice Sephton, Isaac Nape, Yijie Shen, Andrew Forbes
Categories: quant-ph, physics.optics
Published: 2026-03-11 10:24:49+00:00
Abstract: Orbital angular momentum (OAM) entanglement gives access to multiple qubit and high dimensional Hilbert spaces, but is unfortunately susceptible to disturbance, decaying in real-world noisy channels. Here, we show there is an underlying topology arising from OAM entanglement that is robust to such channels, which we demonstrate using atmospheric turbulence -- exemplary of stochastic or chaotic media. Using a quantum channel with various turbulence strengths, we find the OAM topological observable preserved even though the OAM itself is shown to be highly sensitive to the turbulence. We show this is true for mixed states too, with the OAM topology intact even as the purity of the state decreases due to decoherence. Our work offers a new perspective on OAM entanglement preservation, and may easily be extended to other spatial bases, degrees of freedom, as well as complex channels, whether static or dynamic.
