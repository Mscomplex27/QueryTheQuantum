Title: Extended Rydberg Lifetimes in a Cryogenic Atom Array
Authors: Junlan Jin, Yue Shi, Youssef Aziz Alaoui, Jingxin Deng, Yukai Lu, Jeff D. Thompson, Waseem S. Bakr
Categories: physics.atom-ph, cond-mat.quant-gas, quant-ph
Published: 2026-02-05 18:13:34+00:00
Abstract: We report on the realization of a $^{133}$Cs optical tweezer array in a cryogenic blackbody radiation (BBR) environment. By enclosing the array within a 4K radiation shield, we measure long Rydberg lifetimes, up to $406 (36)\,μ$s for the $55 P_{3/2}$ Rydberg state, a factor of 3.3(3) longer than the room-temperature value. We employ single-photon coupling for coherent manipulation of the ground-Rydberg qubit. We measure a small differential dynamic polarizability of the transition, beneficial for reducing dephasing due to light intensity fluctuations. Our results pave the path for advancing neutral-atom two-qubit gate fidelities as their error budgets become increasingly dominated by $T_1$ relaxation of the ground-Rydberg qubit.
